## 3. Print settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Nozzle, layer | 0.4 mm, 0.2 mm |
| Walls | 2 for the tubes (they are thin shells); 3 for the tower, crown, mount and tiles |
| Infill | 15 % gyroid (tiles, mount, crown); tubes and lattice have none |
| Supports | none; the tower braces are 3 mm bars at under 45° |
| Brim | 5 mm on the tubes and the tower sections (tall, small footprint) |
| Minimum layer time | leave the default; the Ø64 and Ø80 tubes are limited by it, not by speed |

Printing time from the slicer: 65 hours; the tower sections are 25 h and the platform tiles 12 h.

## 4. Assembly

1. Tower: stack the four sections, pegs into holes, crown on top. Clip the arm bracket and the wire saddle to the corner post nearest the rocket, bracket at 840 mm above the platform.
2. Rocket: core segments on the launch mount, spigots up, capsule last. Boosters into their pockets, lower then upper; push a strut through each pair of holes.
3. Arm: pin it to the bracket; it swings to rest its yoke on the capsule.
4. Platform: tiles joined with the dovetails, ribs down; launch mount centred; the tower stands 300 mm from the rocket axis.
5. Wire: lay it in the saddle's groove on its way from the yard to the window hook. The hook takes the load.
